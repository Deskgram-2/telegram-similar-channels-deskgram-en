# Telegram Similar Channel Search with Deskgram 2

Similar Channel Search in Deskgram 2 helps you expand a Telegram source map from already trusted communities instead of relying only on keyword guesses. This module works best when you already know a few strong channels in the niche and want to scale discovery around them.

## About the module

| Parameter | What is inside |

## What it can do

- use a seed list of relevant channels as the starting layer;
- find adjacent communities close to the original topic;
- grow the source map faster than repeated manual exploration;
- show progress and result quality in one workspace;
- prepare sources for parsing and outreach workflows.

## Quick start

1. Prepare a small list of channels that already match the niche well.
2. Add them as seed sources in the module.
3. Launch similar-channel search.
4. Filter the discovered results and keep the strongest sources.
5. Move the cleaned list into parsing or growth flows.

## How the scenario works

### Seed communities

The process starts with channels that already fit the niche. Their quality defines how clean and relevant the expansion becomes.

### Expansion

Deskgram 2 searches for neighboring Telegram communities similar to the seed layer. This makes discovery more focused than starting every pass from keywords alone.

### Handoff

After expansion, the source map becomes useful for parsers, invite flows, and route planning inside a larger acquisition funnel.

## When it is especially useful

- when the niche is already clear but the source map is still too small;
- when you have strong seed channels and want to expand from them;
- when manual source expansion is already slowing you down;
- when discovery should lead directly into user parsing or growth.

## Scenario FAQ

### When is this stronger than keyword search?

When you already have real niche signals in the form of strong seed channels and want discovery to stay close to them instead of starting from broad keyword assumptions.

### Should I parse users after this stage or keep expanding first?

Move into [Audience Parser](https://github.com/Deskgram-2/telegram-audience-parser-deskgram-en) when the source map already looks large enough. Keep expanding first if the niche still feels too narrow.

### What affects result quality the most?

The quality of the seed list. Cleaner and more relevant seed channels usually produce cleaner expansion.
